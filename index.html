Samuele Murru is a graphic designer and art director with a deep passion for visual communication. Born and based in Rome, he works across design, branding, communication strategies, and website development. His work is driven by personal passions and a genuine interest in visual research.

He is the co-founder of Visione Telaio, a project that brings together community and cycling culture, and he has gained significant experience as a junior designer at Lovegang, where he contributed to various campaigns, collections, and communication projects.
